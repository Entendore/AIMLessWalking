❓ How To Play

OBJECTIVE

Guide mindless walkers from the yellow spawn arrow to the green exit door. Save the required number to complete each level.

CONTROLS

Click skill button, then click a walker to assign
Right-Click walker to undo last assignment
1-9, 0, A-H select skills directly
P pause · R rewind · Esc deselect
Space pause/resume

SKILLS

☂ Floater — Survives any fall (umbrella deploys)
⛔ Blocker — Makes other walkers turn around
🧱 Builder — Builds 12-step diagonal stairs
⛏ Basher — Digs horizontally through dirt
⬇ Digger — Digs straight down
🧗 Climber — Scales vertical walls
💣 Exploder — Timed 5s explosion
🏊 Swimmer — Survives water
🦘 Jumper — Leaps over small gaps
🪂 Glider — Floats and drifts while falling
🔥 Firewalker — Immune to fire
🛡️ Spikewalker — Immune to spikes
👻 Ghost — Phases through walls briefly
🚀 Rocket — Launches upward
🔀 Splitter — Creates a clone
🌉 Bridge — Builds horizontal platform

TERRAIN

Brown — Dirt (diggable/bashable)
Gray — Steel (indestructible)
Orange — Fire (deadly)
Silver — Spikes (deadly)
Blue — Water (drowns non-swimmers)
Gold — Stars (collect for bonus)
